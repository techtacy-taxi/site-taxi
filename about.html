Who We Are

Taxi & Van Transfers is a professional transfer service based in Lavrio, serving Athens and the wider Attica region around the clock. We are a local team built around a network of experienced, licensed partner drivers — which means whether you need a single sedan for an airport run or a minibus for a whole sailing crew, we can send the right vehicle for the job.

Over the years we have built our reputation on one thing: doing exactly what we promise, on time, every time. That is why travellers from all over the world have rated us 5 out of 5 stars across 240+ Google reviews. We treat every transfer — from a quick port run to a full-day private tour — with the same care.

What Makes Us Different

A real local network

We are based here in the Lavrio and Mesogeia area, not a faceless call centre. We know the roads, the ports, the marinas and the shortcuts — including hard-to-reach villas and remote locations other services avoid.

The right vehicle, every time

Through our partner network we operate sedans, vans and minibuses, comfortably covering everyone from solo travellers to groups of up to 50 — with room for luggage and sailing gear.

Flight & ferry tracking

We monitor your flight or ferry, so if your schedule changes your driver adjusts automatically and is always there waiting for you.

Clear, upfront pricing

You get a fixed quote in advance via WhatsApp — no meters, no surprises, no hidden fees.

Specialists in sailing crews

With Lavrio Port and Olympic Marine on our doorstep, we are a go-to choice for sailing crews who need spacious, on-time transfers for the whole team and their equipment.

Where We Operate

Our network covers Athens and the whole of Attica, with particular strength across the south-east: Lavrio, Keratea, Lagonisi, Saronida, Anavyssos, Markopoulo and Sounio. We handle transfers to and from Athens International Airport (ATH) and the ports of Lavrio, Piraeus and Rafina, as well as door-to-door service to hotels, villas and Airbnb stays.
