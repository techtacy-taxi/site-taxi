About Us

One team, every transfer solution across Athens & Lavrio.

Who We Are

Taxi & Van Transfers is a network of professional, English-speaking partner drivers based in Lavrio and serving the wider Athens region, including Lagonisi, Keratea and Cape Sounio. What began with a passion for reliable, friendly service has grown into a trusted team that travelers rate 5.0 stars on Google, with more than 240 reviews.

We are not a faceless call center and we are not a single car. We are a coordinated team of experienced local drivers who share the same standards: punctuality, clean and comfortable vehicles, fair fixed pricing, and genuine local knowledge of every road, port and neighborhood in our area.

Our Fleet

Because we work as a team, we can match the right vehicle to every journey — whether you are a solo traveler, a family, a sailing crew with extra gear, or a large group heading to a conference. Through our network we provide access to up to 15 taxis and sedans, up to 7 luxury vans, up to 4 minibuses, and 50-seat coaches for the largest groups.

Where We Operate

Athens International Airport, the ports of Lavrio, Piraeus and Rafina, Olympic Marine, and the towns of Lavrio, Lagonisi, Keratea and Sounio. We also run private sightseeing tours to the Acropolis, Cape Sounio, Delphi, Meteora and Argolis.
