Who We Are

Taxi & Van Transfers is a professional transfer service based in Lavrio, serving Athens and the wider Attica region around the clock. We're a local team built around a network of experienced, licensed partner drivers — meaning whether you need a sedan for the airport or a mini bus for an entire crew, we send the right vehicle.

What Sets Us Apart

A genuine local network

We're based right here, in the Lavrio and Mesogeia area — not a faceless call center. We know the roads, the ports, the marinas, even hard-to-reach villas.

The right vehicle, every time

Through our partner network we have sedans, vans and mini buses, comfortably covering everything from solo travelers to groups of up to 50 people.

Flight & ferry tracking

We track your flight or ferry, so your driver adjusts automatically and is always waiting for you.

Clear upfront pricing

You get a fixed quote in advance on WhatsApp — no meters, no hidden charges.

Sailing crew specialists

With Lavrio Port and Olympic Marine right next to us, we're a top choice for crews needing spacious, punctual transfers.

Where We Operate

Our network covers Athens and all of Attica, with particular strength in the southeast: Lavrio, Keratea, Lagonisi, Saronida, Anavyssos, Markopoulo and Cape Sounio. We handle transfers to and from Athens Airport (ATH) and the ports of Lavrio, Piraeus and Rafina, as well as door-to-door service to hotels, villas and Airbnbs.
