游览概述

Meteora is truly a geological phenomenon. Here, massive dark rock pillars rise dramatically from the plains of Thessaly, crowned by ancient Eastern Orthodox monasteries. It is a 联合国教科文组织世界遗产 site and offers some of the most surreal landscapes in Europe.

On this private grand tour, you will travel north through the Greek mainland. Our knowledgeable drivers will ensure a comfortable journey. Upon arriving at Kalambaka, you'll be driven up to the immense rock formations to visit the historic monasteries, witness the breathtaking panoramic views, and learn about the monastic life.

行程亮点

1. Early Departure

Since Meteora is located in central Greece, we begin our day early. Your private driver will pick you up from your Athens hotel for the road trip north.

2. The Greek Mainland

Enjoy the beautiful changing landscapes, passing by the battlefield of Thermopylae (the monument of King Leonidas) and across the fertile Thessalian plain.

3. Arrival at Kalambaka

Reach the picturesque town of Kalambaka, which rests right at the foot of the Meteora rocks. From here, the towering cliffs become visible.

4. 修道院参观 & Sightseeing

We'll drive you safely up the winding roads to visit 2 or 3 of the active monasteries (like Great Meteoron or Varlaam). You will have time to explore and take incredible photos from the best viewpoints.

5. Lunch & Smooth Return

After a traditional Greek lunch in Kalambaka or Kastraki village, you can sit back and relax as we drive you comfortably back to Athens.
