Athens Airport to Lavrio Port Transfer

Direct, fixed-price private transfers — about 35–45 minutes.

A Direct Transfer Built for Travelers and Sailing Crews

Lavrio Port is one of the closest ports to Athens International Airport, and it is the main departure point for ferries to the Cyclades (Kea, Kythnos and beyond) and the base for many sailing charters at Olympic Marine. A private transfer takes roughly 35 to 45 minutes door to door, so you skip the long taxi queues at the airport and arrive relaxed and on time for your ferry or yacht.

We pick you up directly at the arrivals gate, help with your luggage, and drive you straight to your pier or marina. Because we work as a team of drivers with access to a large fleet, we can send a sedan for a couple or a high-capacity van for a full sailing crew with sails, bags and equipment — no struggling to fit everyone into a small car.
