Route Overview

Athens International Airport (ATH) to Lavrio Port is roughly 35–40 km, a drive of about 30–40 minutes depending on traffic. Lavrio is the departure point for ferries to Kea (Tzia), Kythnos and other Cyclades islands, and it is also home to Olympic Marine, one of the largest yacht and sailing bases in Greece.

If you have a ferry to catch or are arriving with a sailing crew, a direct private transfer is by far the simplest option. Your driver meets you in arrivals, helps with your luggage, and takes you straight to the port or marina — no changes, no waiting for a connection, and no stress about your ferry time.

Your Options to Get There

1. Private Taxi or Van Transfer (recommended)

A fixed, door-to-port ride with meet & greet at arrivals. Best for ferry connections, families and sailing crews, as there is plenty of room for luggage and gear. Sedans, vans and minibuses are available for groups of any size.

2. Public Bus (KTEL)

The most economical option, but buses are infrequent, may require a connection, and are not ideal with luggage or a tight ferry schedule. Plan for extra time if you choose this route.

3. Rental Car

Flexible if you want to explore the area, but you will need to handle parking and the return of the vehicle — less convenient if you are heading straight onto a ferry.

What to Expect with a Private Transfer

1. Meet & Greet at Arrivals

Your driver waits for you at the arrivals exit with a name sign. We track your flight, so even if it is delayed, your driver will be there.

2. Luggage & Sailing Gear

We help load your bags into a spacious, air-conditioned vehicle. Travelling with a crew and extra equipment? Just let us know the number of people and bags and we will send the right vehicle.

3. The Drive to Lavrio

A comfortable, direct route through the Mesogeia region to the south-east coast of Attica, with no stops or transfers along the way.

4. Drop-off at the Port or Marina

We take you exactly where you need to be — the ferry gate at Lavrio Port or your berth at Olympic Marine — in good time for check-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Athens Airport from Lavrio Port?

About 35–40 km, roughly a 30–40 minute drive depending on traffic and your exact pickup point.

Is there a bus from the airport to Lavrio Port?

Public KTEL buses serve the wider Lavrio area, but they are infrequent and can require a connection — not ideal with luggage or a fixed ferry departure. A direct transfer is far more reliable.

Which ferries leave from Lavrio?

Lavrio serves ferries to Kea (Tzia) and Kythnos, with seasonal links to other Cyclades islands. It is also a major sailing base (Olympic Marine).

What if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight and adjust the pickup automatically, so your driver is always there when you land.
