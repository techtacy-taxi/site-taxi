Route Overview

Athens International Airport (ATH) to Cape Sounio is roughly 50 km, about 45–60 minutes along the scenic coastal road. Cape Sounio is home to the magnificent Temple of Poseidon, famous for one of the best sunsets in Greece.

Whether you are staying at a Sounio resort or visiting for the sunset, a private transfer lets you travel comfortably and time your arrival perfectly — with the option to combine it with a stop along the Athens Riviera.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Cape Sounio from Athens Airport?

About 50 km, usually a 45–60 minute drive along the coastal road.

Is Sounio worth visiting for the sunset?

Yes — the Temple of Poseidon at sunset is one of the most famous views in Greece. We can time your transfer to arrive before sunset.

Can you wait and bring me back?

Yes. We offer round trips with waiting time, or a full private Sounio tour.

Do you serve Sounio hotels?

Yes, we provide door-to-door transfers to all the Cape Sounio resorts and nearby coastal hotels.
