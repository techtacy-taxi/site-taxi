Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our taxi, van and transfer services in Athens.

How can I book a transfer?

You can book easily and instantly by clicking the WhatsApp button. We're available 24/7 to confirm your booking and answer any questions.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track all flights in real time. Your driver will be waiting at the airport at the actual arrival time, with no extra charge for flight delays.

Are your prices fixed?

Yes, all our prices are fixed and agreed before the transfer. There are no hidden fees, tolls, or extra luggage charges.

Where will the driver meet me?

At the airport, we contact you via WhatsApp and make sure your driver is waiting right at the exit, so you won't have to walk far. For hotels or ports, they'll meet you in the lobby or outside the terminal.

Do you offer private tours?

We have extensive experience with private tours in Athens (Acropolis, Cape Sounio) and other historic regions of Greece (Delphi, Meteora, Argolis). All tours are fully customizable.
