Route Overview

Athens International Airport (ATH) to Lagonisi is roughly 30 km, about 30–40 minutes by car depending on traffic. Lagonisi sits on the beautiful Athens Riviera, surrounded by beach resorts, villas and the coastal road south towards Sounio.

A private transfer takes you straight from arrivals to your hotel, villa or Airbnb — ideal after a long flight, and especially handy for the coastal villas that are tricky to reach by public transport.
