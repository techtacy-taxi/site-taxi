Lavrio Port to Athens

Just stepped off the ferry from Kea or Kythnos? Here is how to get from Lavrio Port to central Athens quickly and comfortably.

Back to Home

Route Overview

Lavrio Port to central Athens is roughly 60 km, a drive of about 50–70 minutes depending on traffic and your exact destination. Lavrio is the arrival point for ferries from Kea (Tzia), Kythnos and other Cyclades islands.

After a ferry crossing, the last thing you want is to hunt for transport at the port. A private transfer means your driver is waiting on arrival, helps with your luggage, and takes you straight to your hotel, the airport or any address in Athens — no connections, no stress.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Lavrio Port from Athens?

Lavrio Port to central Athens is about 60 km, usually a 50–70 minute drive depending on traffic and your destination.

Can you take me from Lavrio Port to the airport?

Yes. We provide direct transfers from Lavrio Port to Athens International Airport (ATH), as well as to the city centre and any address.

Will the driver wait if my ferry is late?

Yes. We monitor ferry arrivals and your driver adjusts to the actual docking time.

Do you carry large luggage?

Yes. We have vans and minibuses with plenty of space for suitcases and sailing equipment.
