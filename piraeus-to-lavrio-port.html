Route Overview

Piraeus Port to Lavrio Port is roughly 55 km, about 50–70 minutes by car depending on traffic. Travellers often need this connection when a cruise or ferry arrives at Piraeus and their next ferry (to Kea or Kythnos) departs from Lavrio.

Port-to-port connections are time-sensitive, so a private transfer is the safest choice. Your driver meets you, handles the luggage, and gets you to Lavrio in good time for check-in — no juggling buses or metro with suitcases.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Lavrio Port from Piraeus?

About 55 km, usually a 50–70 minute drive depending on traffic.

Why would I need this transfer?

Many travellers arrive at Piraeus by cruise or ferry and need to reach Lavrio for an onward ferry to Kea or Kythnos.

Can you guarantee I make my ferry?

We plan the pickup around your departure and monitor timing, giving you the best margin for a safe connection.

Do you handle large groups?

Yes, with vans and minibuses for groups and crews, including luggage and equipment.
