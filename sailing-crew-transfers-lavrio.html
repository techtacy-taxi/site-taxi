Built for Sailing Crews

Lavrio is one of the largest sailing hubs in Greece, home to Olympic Marine and a busy yacht port. We specialise in transfers for sailing crews — which means high-capacity vans that fit your whole team plus heavy bags, sails and equipment, without the squeeze of an ordinary taxi.

From an airport pickup the day you arrive, to provisioning stops on the way to the marina, to getting the crew back to Athens at the end of the charter, we handle the logistics so you can focus on the sailing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you fit a whole sailing crew with luggage?

Yes. That is our speciality — high-capacity vans and minibuses with room for the full crew, bags, sails and equipment.

Do you go directly to Olympic Marine?

Yes, we take you straight to your berth at Olympic Marine or the Lavrio yacht port.

Can you do an airport pickup for the crew?

Yes. We provide Athens Airport to Lavrio transfers with flight tracking, and the return at the end of your charter.

Can you stop for provisioning?

Absolutely. Let us know in advance and we will include a supermarket or supply stop on the way.
