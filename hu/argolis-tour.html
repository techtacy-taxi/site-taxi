Mükéné & Epidaurusz

Step into the Főoldalric epics. Visit the golden city of Mükéné and the acoustic marvel of Epidaurusz.

Vissza az összes túrához

Túra áttekintése

Join us on an unforgettable journey to the Argolisz peninsula, where Greek mythology comes to life. This full-day private tour takes you to some of the most significant archaeological sites of ancient Greece, steeped in the legends of King Agamemnon and the Trojan War.

You will walk through the imposing Lion Gate of Mükéné, explore monumental tholos tombs, stroll through the romantic streets of Nafplio (the first capital of modern Greece), and finish at the breathtaking Sanctuary of Asklepios in Epidaurusz, home to the ancient theater famous for its perfect acoustics.

Mire számíthat

1. Departure & Corinth Canal

Your private driver will pick you up in Athens. Our first major stop will be a breathtaking view of the Corinth Canal, an engineering marvel that connects the Aegean and Ionian seas.

2. Ancient Mükéné

Enter the ancient kingdom of Agamemnon through the legendary Lion Gate. Explore the Cyclopean walls, the Royal Tombs, and the awe-inspiring Treasury of Atreus (Tomb of Agamemnon).

3. Nafplio City

We'll stop at the picturesque coastal city of Nafplio. Enjoy a walk through its charming narrow streets, admire the Bourtzi fortress in the water, and have a traditional Greek lunch.

4. Theater of Epidaurusz

The tour concludes with a visit to the ancient Theater of Epidaurusz, renowned worldwide for its flawless acoustics. Even a whisper on the stage can be heard clearly in the highest seating rows.

5. Return to Athens

After a day full of mythological wonders and magnificent architecture, relax in your premium vehicle as we head back to your accommodation in Athens.
